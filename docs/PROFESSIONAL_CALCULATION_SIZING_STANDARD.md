# Professional Mechanical Calculation and Sizing Standard

## Purpose

Every network segment is solved from the approved project/system design basis. A
declared diameter is not proof of design. The executable record must show its
downstream demand, unit, every evaluated catalogue/rule-table candidate, the
first compliant candidate, capacity, utilization, reserve and provenance.

## Locked execution contract

1. Endpoint demands are architecture/owner/Rule-Book backed and typed.
2. Every segment accumulates all and only its downstream endpoints.
3. A supplied accumulated load is independently recalculated and must match.
4. Candidate rows contain finite increasing capacity and explicit size.
5. Candidates are evaluated deterministically from least capacity upward.
6. The first capacity meeting demand is the only automatic selection.
7. No in-range demand may silently use a larger or smaller automatic size.
8. Out-of-range demand fails; table extension requires governed evidence.
9. The selected capacity, utilization and reserve are recorded per segment.
10. The complete evaluated-candidate history is retained for audit.
11. Load and size units are explicit; parsing or conversion ambiguity stops.
12. Material and material source remain mandatory.
13. Gravity systems retain an explicit slope basis.
14. Plan, riser, calculation and schedule consume the same calculation record.
15. Independent calculation QA recomputes the selection, not producer PASS data.
16. Missing professional evidence is `INPUT_REQUIRED`; contradiction is `FAIL`.
17. Positive and destructive regressions cover load and sizing tamper.
18. Submission remains blocked until every applicable record passes.

The broad physical-limit, geometry/equivalent-length, pump/equipment, sensitivity
and independent-engineer gates remain unchanged. This capability does not invent
project facts and does not use private reference drawings during generation.
