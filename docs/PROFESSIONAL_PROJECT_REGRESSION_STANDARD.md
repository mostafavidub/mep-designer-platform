# Professional Project Regression Standard

Regression proves that an identified candidate build improves its intended defect without degrading any project, critical engineering invariant or exact deliverable. Successful file creation or a green health endpoint is insufficient.

Each privacy-safe case seals the architecture hash, questionnaire answers, approved Manifest, Rule Book and manufacturer-data hashes. Mechanical references remain sealed during generation and may be opened only after the candidate artifacts are sealed. Development, validation and sealed-evaluation cohorts are disjoint.

Every case executes at least twice under the same Git/dependency/runtime identity. The harness retains DXF, PDF, calculations, schedules, Manifest, QA and renders; compares scope, graph identity, independently recomputed engineering values, architecture preservation, exact-DXF semantics and sheet visuals; executes destructive/metamorphic and recovery tests; and rejects undeclared numeric tolerances.

All eighteen controls must score exactly 100/100. Every critical gate must remain PASS and every per-project score must be greater than or equal to its approved baseline. An improved mean cannot hide a weaker individual project. Completion additionally requires a real E2E artifact from Staging at the exact merged SHA.

Private source drawings and customer identifiers are never committed. Baselines store hashes and privacy-safe semantic inventories. A known baseline defect is never promoted into an acceptance rule.
