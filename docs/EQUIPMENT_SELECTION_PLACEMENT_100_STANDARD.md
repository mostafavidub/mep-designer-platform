# Mechanical Equipment Selection and Placement 100 Standard

**Rule:** `MEP-EQUIP-002`  
**Contract:** `equipment-selection-placement/1`  
**Status:** LOCKED

## Acceptance

Mechanical equipment is selected only from calculated project demand and an
immutable official manufacturer record. Placement is accepted only when hosted
by authoritative architecture, clear for installation and service, safe,
connected to every required network and reconciled across Plan, Riser, Schedule
and the exact reopened DXF. All eighteen controls must explicitly pass and the
weighted score must equal 100. Missing, unknown or skipped evidence fails closed.

## Eighteen controls

1. Typed room and level model (5).
2. Existing/proposed equipment classification (3).
3. Complete project design basis (6).
4. Space-by-space demand calculation (8).
5. Water-service equipment duties (4).
6. Ventilation equipment duties (4).
7. Official immutable catalogue evidence (7).
8. Deterministic compliant-model selection (6).
9. Capacity and permitted oversizing reconciliation (8).
10. Evidence-backed candidate locations (4).
11. Valid architectural host and no obstruction (7).
12. Installation, replacement and service clearance (6).
13. Exactly-once required network connections (7).
14. Safety and code clearances (6).
15. Multi-criteria placement ranking (4).
16. Exact Plan/Riser/Schedule identity and capacity (6).
17. Per-equipment, per-sheet visual QA (5).
18. Exact reopened-output identity and immutability (4).

The weights total 100. No global capacity pattern or value observed in a
mechanical reference drawing may be used as a generation default. Existing
outputs must be regenerated to acquire this evidence. Submission Ready remains
subject to the project inputs and responsible engineer review required by the
other locked gates.
